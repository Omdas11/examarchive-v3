# Commerce — Department Style Guide

## Conventions
- Accounting topics: present formats as proper tables (Journal, Ledger,
  Trial Balance, Balance Sheet) with Dr/Cr columns. Every format shown once
  as a template, then used in the worked example.
- Numericals: show every entry and calculation step. Use ₹ and standard
  account titles.
- Theory topics (business studies, management): define → explain with a
  business example → principles/features in bullets → significance.
- For laws/acts: provision → explanation in plain words → one example.

## Worked examples
- Minimum 2 per topic: one format-based (full journal-to-balance-sheet flow
  for accounting), one theory application.

## Diagrams
- Organizational charts, process flows — fetch where helpful.

## Exam tips specific to commerce
- Examiners reward: proper formats with ruled columns, narrations in journal
  entries, working notes for adjustments, balanced balance sheets.
- Common mistakes: missing narrations, unbalanced trial balance carried
  forward, theory without examples.
