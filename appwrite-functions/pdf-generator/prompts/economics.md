# Economics — Department Style Guide

## Conventions
- Every curve/law: statement → diagram via [FETCH_IMAGE: ...] (labeled axes,
  curves, equilibrium points) → explanation of the diagram → assumptions →
  exceptions/limitations.
- Diagrams are mandatory for micro topics (demand/supply, cost curves,
  market structures). Describe what shifts each curve and why.
- Numericals (elasticity, national income, multiplier): formula → substitution
  → answer with interpretation ("elastic demand means...").
- Macro topics: define the aggregate → show the mechanism (e.g., how the
  multiplier process iterates) → policy implication.
- Distinguish clearly: real vs nominal, short run vs long run, movement along
  vs shift of a curve.

## Worked examples
- Minimum 2 per topic: one diagram-based explanation, one numerical with
  economic interpretation.

## Exam tips specific to economics
- Examiners reward: labeled diagrams with explanations (a diagram without
  explanation earns little), definitions before analysis, policy conclusions.
- Common mistakes: unlabeled axes, confusing shift vs movement, no
  interpretation of numerical results.
