# Assamese (অসমীয়া) — Department Style Guide

## Language
- Write notes primarily in Assamese, with key literary terms also given in
  English in parentheses on first use.
- Quotations from texts stay in original Assamese.

## Conventions
- For each text/author: brief author introduction (era, literary movement —
  e.g., Jonaki era, Assam Sahitya Sabha context), then theme-wise analysis.
- Literary analysis per topic: মূলভাৱ (central idea) → বিষয়বস্তু সংক্ষেপ
  (summary) → চৰিত্ৰ বিশ্লেষণ (character analysis) → ভাষা আৰু শৈলী
  (language and style) → সমালোচনামূলক মূল্যায়ন (critical evaluation).
- Always include 3-4 memorable quotations per major text with one-line
  explanations.
- Literary history: period characteristics in bullets, major authors with
  one-line contributions.

## Worked examples
- Model answers: one short and one long question per topic, written as exam
  answers — introduction, body points, conclusion.

## Exam tips specific to Assamese
- Examiners reward: quotations, era/movement context, structured answers.
- Common mistakes: plot retelling without analysis, missing quotations.
