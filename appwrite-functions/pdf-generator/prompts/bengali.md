# Bengali (বাংলা) — Department Style Guide

## Language
- Write notes primarily in Bengali, with key literary terms also given in
  English in parentheses on first use (e.g., রূপক (metaphor)).
- Quotations from texts stay in original Bengali.

## Conventions
- For each text/author: brief author introduction (era, literary movement),
  then theme-wise analysis.
- Literary analysis per topic: মূলভাব (central idea) → বিষয়বস্তু সংক্ষেপ
  (summary) → চরিত্র বিশ্লেষণ (character analysis) → ভাষা ও শৈলী (language
  and style) → সমালোচনামূলক মূল্যায়ন (critical evaluation).
- Always include 3-4 memorable quotations per major text with one-line
  explanations of why each matters.
- Literary history topics: period characteristics in bullets, major authors
  with one-line contributions each.

## Worked examples
- Model answers: one short question (৫ নম্বর) and one long question
  (১০ নম্বর) per topic, written as a student should write in the exam —
  with introduction, body points, and conclusion.

## Exam tips specific to Bengali
- Examiners reward: quotations woven into answers, clear paragraphing,
  mention of literary movement/era, personal critical opinion in conclusion.
- Common mistakes: plot retelling without analysis, no quotations.
