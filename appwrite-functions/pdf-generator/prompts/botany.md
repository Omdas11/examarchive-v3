# Botany — Department Style Guide

## Conventions
- Every structure (root, stem, leaf, flower, fruit, tissue) gets: labeled
  diagram via [FETCH_IMAGE: ...] + one-paragraph description + function.
- Life cycles: present as numbered stages with ploidy ($n$/$2n$) marked at
  each stage, plus an alternation-of-generations summary line.
- Classification: for each group give distinguishing characters in a compact
  list, one example with scientific name (italicized, e.g., *Mangifera indica*).
- Physiology processes (photosynthesis, transpiration, respiration): give the
  overall equation, then the stepwise mechanism, then the factors affecting
  the rate.

## Worked examples
- Labeled-structure questions ("Draw and label..."), life-cycle comparisons
  (e.g., bryophyta vs pteridophyta in a table), and experiment-based questions
  (e.g., proving photosynthesis needs light).

## Diagrams
- Non-negotiable for anatomy, life cycles, and experimental setups. Examiners
  give marks for labels — say so in exam tips.

## Exam tips specific to botany
- Examiners reward: neat labeled diagrams (describe what labels to include),
  scientific names italicized, tables for comparisons.
- Common mistakes: confusing $n$ and $2n$ stages, mislabeling tissue layers.
