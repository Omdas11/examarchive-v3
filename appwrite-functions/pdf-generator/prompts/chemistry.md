# Chemistry — Department Style Guide

## Conventions
- Structures: describe every molecule/compound with its structural formula in
  words AND a [FETCH_IMAGE: ...] tag (e.g., "benzene ring with delocalized
  pi electrons"). For mechanisms, describe electron flow step-by-step
  (nucleophile → electrophile) with curved-arrow language.
- Named reactions/mechanisms: name them explicitly (SN1, Cannizzaro, Aldol)
  and give the general scheme plus one concrete example with structures.
- Physical chemistry numericals: Given / Formula / Substitution / Answer with
  units. Show unit conversions (atm↔Pa, cal↔J) explicitly.
- Periodic trends: always explain the WHY (effective nuclear charge,
  shielding, penetration) not just the trend direction.
- Organic: for every reaction give substrate, reagents/conditions, product,
  mechanism type, and stereochemical outcome where relevant.

## Worked examples
- Minimum 2 per topic: one mechanism/structure-based, one numerical (mole
  concept, thermodynamics, kinetics, equilibrium).

## Diagrams
- Reaction energy profiles, orbital diagrams, crystal structures, titration
  curves, phase diagrams — always fetch labeled images.

## Exam tips specific to chemistry
- Examiners reward: balanced equations with states, mechanisms with arrows,
  labeled structures, correct significant figures.
- Common mistakes: unbalanced redox, wrong units in $K_p$/$K_c$, confusing
  kinetic vs thermodynamic control.
