# Political Science — Department Style Guide

## Conventions
- Concepts: definition → key thinkers associated → features/elements in
  bullets → contemporary Indian example.
- Thinkers: context (era, tradition) → core arguments → criticism → relevance
  today.
- Constitutional/legal topics: article/provision number → what it says in
  plain words → significance → landmark case or amendment where relevant.
- Always connect theory to Indian politics (institutions, elections, federalism)
  — examiners expect applied answers, not just Western theory.

## Worked examples
- Model answers: one short note and one essay-type answer per topic, with
  introduction, structured body (thinkers/provisions/examples), and a
  balanced conclusion.

## Exam tips specific to political science
- Examiners reward: article numbers, thinker names with arguments, current
  examples, structured multi-dimensional answers.
- Common mistakes: theory without examples, wrong article numbers, one-sided
  narratives.
